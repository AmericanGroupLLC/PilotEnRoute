Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 11, 2026  |  Last Updated: June 11, 2026  |  Version: 1.0

American Group LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the PilotEnRoute mobile and web application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our app. Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of the application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Pilot Profile: Name, pilot certificate number, certificate type (Student, Private, Instrument, Commercial, ATP), medical class and expiration date.
  • Logbook Entries: Flight dates, departure/arrival airports (ICAO identifiers), aircraft tail numbers, flight times, and pilot-in-command hours.
  • Flight Plans: Route waypoints, departure/destination airports, planned altitude, and aircraft type.
  • Notification Preferences: Your selected reminder schedules for medical renewals, BFR, currency requirements, and FAA alerts.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and app version.
  • Location Data: Approximate GPS location (when permission is granted) used solely to retrieve local METAR/TAF weather data and display nearby airports. Location data is not stored on our servers.
  • Usage Analytics: Anonymous app usage statistics (screens viewed, features used) to improve the app experience. No personally identifiable information is included.
  • Crash Reports: Anonymized crash logs to diagnose and fix technical issues.

1.3 Third-Party Data

  • Aviation Weather Center (aviationweather.gov): Public METAR, TAF, PIREP, and SIGMET data retrieved on-demand. No user data is sent to this service.
  • OpenSky Network (opensky-network.org): Public ADS-B flight tracking data. No user data is sent to this service.
  • RotatePilot API: Aviation knowledge questions and airport data. No user data is sent to this service.
  • Google AdMob: If you are a free-tier user, Google AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the PilotEnRoute application and its features.
  • To send you push notifications for pilot currency reminders, medical renewal alerts, and FAA TFR/SIGMET notifications (only if you grant notification permission).
  • To personalize your experience based on your pilot certificate level.
  • To process in-app purchases for Premium subscriptions via Apple App Store or Google Play Store.
  • To improve the app through anonymous analytics and crash reporting.
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations.

3. Data Storage and Security

All logbook entries, flight plans, and pilot profile data are stored locally on your device using encrypted SQLite storage. We do not transmit your personal aviation data to our servers. You are responsible for backing up your device data.

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS for all API communications and device-level encryption for stored data.

4. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are processed entirely through Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android). We do not collect or store your payment card information. Subscription management, billing, and refunds are handled by Apple or Google according to their respective terms of service.

5. Advertising

Free-tier users may see advertisements served by Google AdMob. These ads may use your advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android) to show relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings. Premium subscribers do not see advertisements.

6. Children's Privacy

PilotEnRoute is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and Correction: You can view and edit your pilot profile and logbook data directly within the app at any time.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete all app data by uninstalling the application or using the "Delete All Data" option in Settings.
  • Notifications: You can disable push notifications at any time through your device's notification settings or within the app's Notifications screen.
  • Location: You can revoke location permission at any time through your device settings. This will disable weather-at-current-location features.
  • Advertising: You can opt out of personalized ads through your device's privacy settings (Limit Ad Tracking on iOS, Opt out of Ads Personalization on Android).

8. Third-Party Links and Affiliate Products

The PilotEnRoute Pilot Store contains affiliate links to third-party retailers (Amazon, Sporty's, etc.). When you click these links, you will be directed to external websites governed by their own privacy policies. We may earn a commission on qualifying purchases at no extra cost to you.

9. International Users (ICARA / International Students)

If you are an international student pilot using the ICARA module, please note that TSA AFSP application links direct you to official U.S. government websites (fts.tsa.dhs.gov). We do not collect or transmit any TSA application data.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy and, for material changes, by sending a push notification through the app. Your continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
