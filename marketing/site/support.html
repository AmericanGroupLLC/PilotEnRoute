Frequently asked

Can I use this for navigation in flight?

No. PilotEnRoute is not approved for navigation and must never be a primary or sole source for a flight operation. It is a study and planning tool.

Is the weather official?

No. It comes from public aviation sources and is for situational awareness while planning. Always get an official weather briefing.

Do I need an account?

No. Tap "Continue as guest". Sign-in only syncs your logbook across devices.

Is the study material FAA-approved?

No. It is study material we prepared. It is not certified by the FAA or any aviation authority, and it does not replace an approved course or your instructor.

Will the app remind me before my medical expires?

Yes, if you add the expiry date and allow notifications. Reminders use inexact alarms, so they arrive around the time you set rather than to the second.

Where is my logbook stored?

On your device, unless you sign in for sync. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes it.
