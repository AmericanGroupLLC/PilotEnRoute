Thanks for reviewing PilotEnRoute.

PilotEnRoute is an aviation STUDY and record-keeping app, not a navigation or avionics product - the listing, the terms and the in-app disclaimer all say so. Sign-in is OPTIONAL: tap "Continue as guest" on the login screen and every feature is available. No demo account is needed.

Test instructions:
1. Launch the app and step through onboarding.
2. On the login screen tap "Continue as guest" - this is the intended review path and no account is required.
3. Open Education and start any subject, then take an exam and review the debrief.
4. Open Tools and use the E6B flight computer.
5. Open Logbook and add an entry, then open Endorsements and Documents.
6. Open Checklists and create a checklist.
7. Open Weather to see live conditions pulled from public aviation sources.
8. Notification reminders (daily quiz, medical expiry) use INEXACT alarms; the app requests no exact-alarm permission.

Privacy:
• Data collected: Your logbook entries, endorsements, documents, checklists and study progress, held on your device. Sign-in is optional; if you use it, your email is held by our authentication provider.
• Network: Public aviation weather and airport data sources. No server of our own is required for the app to function.
• Location: Used only to show weather and airports near you, while the app is in use. You can decline and search by identifier instead.
• Tracking: None. The app does not read the advertising identifier and shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt.

If you need anything else, please email contact@safecodeg.com and we will respond within one business day.
