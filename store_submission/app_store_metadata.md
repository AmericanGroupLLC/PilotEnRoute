```
✈️ PilotEnRoute — Your Complete Pilot Training Hub

Whether you're a student pilot working toward your Private Pilot License or an ATP captain maintaining currency, PilotEnRoute has everything you need in one powerful, beautifully designed app.

🛩️ AI FLIGHT SIMULATOR
Practice real-world scenarios with our lightweight AI-powered flight simulator. Choose from 6 aircraft types including Cessna 172, Piper Cherokee, Cirrus SR22, and more. Train mountain approaches, crosswind landings, IFR departures, and emergency procedures — all without leaving the ground.

🌤️ LIVE AVIATION WEATHER
Real-time METAR, TAF, PIREPs, SIGMETs, and winds aloft data directly from the FAA Aviation Weather Center. Instant VFR/IFR/MVFR condition badges for any airport worldwide. Search any ICAO identifier for instant weather.

📝 FAA EXAM PREPARATION
3,500+ FAA knowledge test questions covering Private Pilot, Instrument Rating, Commercial Pilot, and ATP certificates. Full timed 60-question exam simulations with detailed explanations. Study flashcards with spaced repetition across 8 aviation categories.

📓 DIGITAL LOGBOOK
FAA-compliant digital logbook with automatic currency calculations. Track total time, PIC, night, instrument, cross-country, and simulator hours. Export to PDF for your CFI or airline application.

🗺️ FLIGHT PLANNER
Build complete ICAO-format flight plans with route waypoints, fuel calculations, and weather-aware routing. Save and share flight plans for any trip.

🔍 LIVE FLIGHT TRACKER
Track any aircraft by tail number using OpenSky Network ADS-B data. See real-time position, altitude, speed, and flight path on an interactive map.

🚁 DRONE EDUCATION
Complete FAA Part 107 study guide for commercial drone pilots. Covers airspace classification, weather minimums, LAANC authorization, and remote pilot certificate requirements.

🔔 SMART NOTIFICATIONS
Never fly out of currency again. PilotEnRoute sends personalized reminders for:
• FAA Medical Certificate renewal (Class 1, 2, 3)
• Biennial Flight Review (BFR)
• Instrument Proficiency Check (IPC)
• Passenger-carrying currency
• Night currency
• TFR and SIGMET alerts

⌚ GARMIN INTEGRATION
Connect with Garmin Connect IQ wearables and Garmin aviation devices. Quick links to ForeFlight, SkyVector, and other leading aviation services.

🌍 INTERNATIONAL STUDENTS (ICARA)
Complete guide for international student pilots training in the USA. TSA AFSP application walkthrough, visa requirements, approved flight school finder, and FAA certificate reciprocity information.

✍️ FAA ENDORSEMENTS
Complete library of all FAA endorsement templates per AC 61-65. Student solo, cross-country, practical test, and instrument endorsements ready to use.

🏆 CAREER PATHWAYS
Visual career roadmaps from Student Pilot to Airline Captain. Explore airline, corporate, military, and charter career paths with hour requirements and salary data.

🛒 PILOT GEAR STORE
Curated affiliate store with top-rated pilot headsets (Bose A30, Lightspeed Zulu), GPS units (Garmin Aera 660), flight bags, tablets, and training materials.

📰 AVIATION NEWS
Live aviation news from AOPA, AVweb, and FlightGlobal. Stay current with FAA rulemaking, accident reports, and industry developments.

FREE & PREMIUM TIERS
• Free: AI Simulator, Live Weather, Basic Logbook, Flashcards, Drone Education, News
• Premium Monthly ($4.99/mo): Ad-free + Full Exam Simulation + Unlimited Logbook + Flight Planner
• Premium Annual ($34.99/yr): Best value — save 42%

⚠️ DISCLAIMER: PilotEnRoute is an educational tool only. Not for use during actual flight operations. Always consult official FAA sources and a certified flight instructor.
```
