Before every flight you ask two questions: am I legal for this one, and is today inside my own limits? PilotEnRoute answers both on one card.

Log each flight with the things the regulations count: day and night takeoffs and full-stop landings, approaches, holds, night, instrument and cross-country time. PilotEnRoute works out your passenger currency (day and night), IFR currency, flight review and medical (including BasicMed) from your own entries and dates, and shows the date each one lapses and what you need to fix it.

Set your personal minimums once, for day and for night. Pick your airport and PilotEnRoute pulls the latest METAR and TAF from the Aviation Weather Center. It finds the runway with the least crosswind and shows each number next to your limit.

Search your logbook, see totals for the last 30, 90 and 365 days, and export it as PDF or CSV. Reminders arrive before anything lapses. The Kit has an E6B, weight and balance, fuel and flight planning, representative checklists you can add your own items to, your documents with their expiry dates, airport details and links to FAA terminal procedures.

No account. Your logbook stays on your phone.

PilotEnRoute is a ground planning and record-keeping tool. It is not approved for navigation and does not replace an official weather briefing, your POH/AFM or your instructor. Every go/no-go decision is yours. PilotEnRoute is not affiliated with or endorsed by the FAA, NOAA or any government agency; it reads their public data.

PilotEnRoute is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Where consent is required you are asked first, and you can change it in Settings > Privacy choices.
