## Privacy answers (both dashboards)

- **Data collected:** Your logbook entries, endorsements, documents, checklists and study progress, held on your device. Sign-in is optional; if you use it, your email is held by our authentication provider.
- **Network calls:** Public aviation weather and airport data sources. No server of our own is required for the app to function.
- **Location:** Used only to show weather and airports near you, while the app is in use. You can decline and search by identifier instead.
- **Tracking / IDFA:** None. The app does not read the advertising identifier and shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
- **Children:** Not directed at children under 13; we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Open items

- Phone screenshots are not generated - run ./tools/capture_store_shots.sh on macOS with Xcode.
- The App Review phone number is a placeholder 555 number and must be replaced with a reachable line.
- USE_EXACT_ALARM and SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM were removed and the three notification call sites switched to inexactAllowWhileIdle - Play grants exact-alarm permissions only to alarm-clock and calendar apps, which a quiz and medical-expiry reminder are not. Reminders now arrive within a window rather than to the second.
- README.md:5 calls the app a "100% production-ready Flutter aviation platform" and README.md:13-14 mark Android "Google Play ready" and iOS "App Store ready". Neither is supportable as written: store-kit/validate.sh measures 0 phone screenshots against Play's minimum of 2, and no iOS artifact has been built or verified (flutter doctor on the build host reports no Xcode toolchain, so that check is NOT RUN rather than passing). Correct the README or produce the artifacts.
- AndroidManifest still declares READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE and WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE. Play flags WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on modern targets; confirm both are still needed or remove them.
- Confirm the not-for-navigation disclaimer is shown on first run, not only in the listing and terms.
