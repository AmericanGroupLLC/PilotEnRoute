Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-28 · Last updated 2026-08-28 · Applies to PilotEnRoute for iOS and Android.

PilotEnRoute is published by American Group LLC. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what choices you have. Where the honest answer is “nothing leaves your device”, this policy says so plainly rather than reserving rights we do not exercise.

2. What the app does not collect

  • Your certificate or medical numbers - the app stores what you type, on your device, and transmits none of it
  • Your contacts, photos or microphone
  • Any advertising or cross-app tracking identifier
  • Flight tracking data tied to your identity

3. Location

Used only to show weather and airports near you, while the app is in use. You can decline and search by identifier instead.

4. Third parties

Weather and airport information come from public aviation data sources, which see the request and its originating IP. Optional sign-in is handled by Supabase. No third party receives a profile of you from us.

5. Children

Not directed at children under 13; we do not knowingly collect their data.

6. Your rights and deleting your data

Delete an entry in the app to remove it. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes your whole local record. If you signed in, email us to have that account deleted. You can also email contact@americangroup.ai with any request about your data; we respond within one business day.

7. Retention and security

Your records stay on your device until you delete them. Network requests use HTTPS.

8. Changes to this policy

Material changes are published on this page with an updated “last updated” date before they take effect.
