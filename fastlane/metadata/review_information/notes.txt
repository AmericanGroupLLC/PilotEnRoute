WHAT THIS APP DOES
PilotEnRoute is a pilot's logbook that works out currency and checks live weather against the pilot's own minimums. It is a ground tool, not for use in flight. There is no account and no sign-in; the logbook is stored on the device only.

HOW TO REACH THE MAIN FEATURES
On first launch, choose a home airport (e.g. KPAO), enter your medical details and set your personal minimums for day and night. The Today tab shows the Go Card: legality for the planned flight (passengers / night / IFR), worked out on the device from logged flights, and the latest METAR/TAF from aviationweather.gov compared with your minimums, including crosswind on the best runway. Tap "Log this flight" to add an entry. The Logbook tab has search, totals and PDF/CSV export; Weather shows METAR/TAF for any airport; Kit holds ground tools (E6B, weight and balance, fuel, flight planner, checklists, documents, airport details, links to FAA terminal procedures).

EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Aviation Weather Center (aviationweather.gov, NOAA): METAR, TAF and airport data. Public API, no account.
- Google AdMob: banner ads in the Logbook list and on the Weather tab. Where consent is required, Google's consent form is shown first; Settings > Privacy choices reopens it.
- Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports.
- FAA and eCFR pages open in the browser from links; the app does not download charts.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Nothing is remote-configured, hidden or region-dependent.

THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Weather and airport data are public US government data (NOAA/FAA). Regulation references cite 14 CFR. No licensed third-party content.
