PilotEnRoute

Train smarter. Fly safer.

PilotEnRoute is a Flutter aviation training companion: FAA ground school, Part 107 drone material, an E6B, a digital logbook and planning tools - built for study on the ground.

What it does

  • FAA ground school — Study by subject, with exams, flashcards and debriefs.
  • Part 107 drone training — Remote pilot material alongside the manned syllabus.
  • E6B and planning tools — The flight computer, plus fuel and weight planning.
  • Digital logbook — Hours, endorsements and documents in one place.
  • Checklists — Build and follow your own.
  • Study tool, not avionics — Not approved for navigation; verify against official sources.
